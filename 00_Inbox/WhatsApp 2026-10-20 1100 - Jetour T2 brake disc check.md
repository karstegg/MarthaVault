# Jetour T2 – brake disc/pad check during tyre balancing

Related: [[Vehicles]]

## Transcript
"So I'm just doing the balancing on my tyres. I just had a look at the brake discs front and rear on the right hand side. They look similar on the left. So what do you think of this?"

## Images
1. **Front RH, from inside** – disc with caliper, wheel off on balancer; hub nut with blue torque-paint mark; light surface rust on hub face; outer pad visible, thickness hard to judge from the angle.
2. **Rear RH, edge-on** – rear disc and caliper, handbrake cable; disc friction surface looks even, slight rust on the edge; pad material visible but thin-looking at the angle.
3. **Front RH, pad/caliper** – pad stamped `F26-3502080LB / 3502AC3 CAC7241I / F823 J E115 / L.B.N` (CAC logo); wear sensor tip visible; disc has a faint wear lip/step at outer edge, hub face rust normal, no heavy scoring visible.
4. **Close-up (motion-blurred), disc edge in caliper** – pad has material left; disc edge shows a slight lip; no obvious cracks or blueing.

## Assessment (from photos only)
- Nothing alarming: no deep grooves, cracking, blue heat marks, or pad down to backing plate.
- Faint outer lip on the disc is normal wear; the real test is thickness. Photos can't confirm pad thickness or disc minimum.
- Pad thickness: replace at ~3 mm friction material; fine above ~5 mm. Disc: measure with a micrometer against the MIN TH stamp on the disc hat/edge; replace if at/below it or lip is >~1 mm.
- Compare left vs right for even wear (Greg says similar) – sticking slider/caliper if one side is much thinner.
