# Wan2.6 R2V Video Generation Model

**Author:** Wan (@Alibaba_Wan) - Alibaba's Tongyi Lab
**Website:** https://wan.video

## Key Capabilities

- **Character Replication** - Upload 5-second reference video to replicate people, animals, animated characters, or objects into new videos
- **Multi-character Support** - Single and multi-character video generation
- **Audio-Visual Sync** - Replicates appearance AND voice with synchronized output including music, sound effects, and speech

## Potential Applications

- Training video creation with consistent presenters
- Documentation videos with animated characters
- Multilingual content with voice cloning
