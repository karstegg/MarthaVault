# Model Compression (KDD 2006)

**Authors**: Cristian Bucilă, Rich Caruana, Alexandru Niculescu-Mizil (Cornell University)

**Screenshot**: ![[2025-12-10 – Model Compression Paper Screenshot.jpg]]

**Paper**: Search for "Model Compression Bucila Caruana 2006" or check ACM Digital Library

---

## Key Points (from screenshot)

- Foundational paper on **knowledge distillation** / model compression
- Shows how to compress complex ensemble models into smaller, faster models with comparable performance
- Uses ensemble to label large unlabeled dataset, then trains smaller neural net on this labeled data
- MUNGE method for generating synthetic training cases
- Claims 1000x smaller models with similar performance
